Islands of Pain

A turn-based hex strategy game for 2–8 players. Claim an island, grow an economy, raise an army, and be the last castle standing.

⬇ Download for Windows How to play

Windows 64-bit · no installer, just unzip and run · free

What it is

Slow scheming, one turn at a time.

Claim your ground

Choose where your castle goes on turn one. Position is the first real decision you make.

Feed your army

Farms and mines fund everything. Let food run dry and your troops starve — literally.

Break their walls

Swordsmen, archers, walls and gates. Only one thing wins: the enemy castle in ruins.

2–8 players

Free-for-all online with a room code, over a LAN, or solo against bots.

Getting started

About a minute from download to first turn.

  1. Download and unzip Keep the .exe and its files together in one folder.
  2. Run the game Windows may warn about an unrecognised app — choose More info → Run anyway. It is unsigned, not malicious.
  3. Play solo first Pick LAN / Direct → Host Game, press Add random bot, then Start Game. No setup, no server.
  4. Play with friends Choose Play Online. One of you creates a room and shares the four-letter code; everyone else joins with it.

How to play

Everything you need for your first game.

♛

The goal: destroy every other player's castle. Lose your own and you are out. The last player with a castle wins.

1. Your first turn: place your castle

You begin with 50 food, 30 stone, and an empty board.

Highlighted hexes show where your castle can go — click one. A worker appears beside it and your turn ends immediately. That is the entire first turn. You must keep some distance from other castles, so nobody can drop one on top of you.

2. Taking a turn

Players act one at a time. When it is your turn, you can issue as many legal actions as you like, then press End Turn. Each unit may move once, attack once and build once per turn — so a soldier can move and attack, and a worker can move and build.

Click any hex to inspect it in the bottom-right panel: what is there, who owns it, its health, and what your unit still has left to do.

3. Units

Workers are trained at your castle; soldiers at a barracks. One unit per building per turn, and new units cannot act until your next turn.

5. Food and stone

At the start of your turn your farms and mines pay out, and then your units eat. Every unit costs food every single turn.

Here is the number that decides most games: a farm produces 5 food, and five workers eat exactly that. An army has to be paid for.

!

If your food drops below zero, your units starve. Each one loses 20% of its maximum health per turn and they can die of it. The food counter turns red to warn you.

Climbing back above zero stops the damage — but nothing heals.

6. How to actually win

  1. Build a mine early Stone is the real bottleneck. Your starting 30 buys very little, and without a mine you simply stop being able to build.
  2. Then farms — before soldiers Troops you cannot feed will starve. Budget roughly one farm per soldier you intend to keep.
  3. Add a barracks Swordsmen to break things, archers to whittle from safety.
  4. March on a castle 100 health means ten hits from a single swordsman. Bring a group, or you will never finish the job.
  5. Do not leave home undefended Walls and gates buy time, and an unguarded castle is the easiest target on the board.

In short: economy first, army second, siege third. Rushing straight to soldiers is the most common way to lose — they starve before they arrive.

Good to know

  • Two units can never share a hex, and buildings block movement — including your own walls. Only your gate lets your units pass.
  • There is no fog of war: you can see the whole board at all times.
  • Archers ignore line of sight — anything within 5 hexes is a valid target.
  • In a free-for-all, everyone else is an enemy. There are no alliances.
  • If a player disconnects they are eliminated, and the game carries on.

Controls

Everything is mouse-driven except panning.

Left clickSelect one of your units, then click a highlighted hex to move (blue) or attack (red).
Right clickOn your castle or barracks: train units. With a worker selected, on an empty hex: build.
Middle drag / W A S DPan the board.
End TurnBottom-right button. Turns also end automatically when the timer runs out.

Before you download

!

This is an early build. Graphics are placeholder coloured shapes, there is no sound, and balance is unfinished — long games can stall without a decisive winner if everyone neglects their economy.

Online play runs through a free-tier server that sleeps when idle, so the first connection after a quiet spell may take a minute or need a retry. Solo and LAN games need no server at all.
